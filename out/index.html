Documents Usually Needed

Keep these standard papers ready to accelerate your bank file login and sanction.

🪪

1. Identity & Current Address Proof

PAN Card (mandatory for tax KYC), Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID for bank physical verification.

📄

2. Income Verification Documents

Salaried: Last 3 months payslips & Form 16. Self-Employed: Last 2-3 years ITR with Computation of Income.

🏦

3. Banking Statements

Latest 6 months continuous bank account statements showing salary credits or business turnover.

📜

4. Property & Legal Title Papers

For Home Loan & LAP: Registered Sale Agreement, 30-year Encumbrance Certificate (EC), link documents & approved plan.

🏭

5. Business Proof (For Commercial & MSME)

GST Registration Certificate, Udyam MSME Registration, Partnership Deed or MOA, and Shop Establishment Act license.

⚠️ Note: Exact document checklist depends on the specific bank and your employment or property profile.
